Started with one oven and a starter named after a street.

Maison Levain began in a rented kitchen with a single deck oven and a sourdough culture we still feed every morning. We mill a portion of our own flour, keep the ingredient list short, and bake in small batches so nothing sits under a heat lamp. Everything you see in the case was made before sunrise, by hand, by people who live in this neighborhood.
